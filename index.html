01

The Mission

My core mission with Prayog Shala is to personally go to government schools and teach students in Grades 6th through 9th science concepts through observation and fun, interactive experiments.

I want to help students build strong scientific visualization and intuition, while simultaneously promoting STEM to young girls and actively breaking traditional gender stereotypes that restrict their potential.

Why I Focus on Hands-On Observation

I have always had a deep passion for STEM and engineering. I realized that most science concepts are taught with maximum theory and minimal practical examples, missing the opportunity to kindle true visual intuition.

02

Problems I Recognized & My Action Plan

How I connect these real-world issues directly to my mission

Problem 1: Gender Stereotypes Restricting Girls

Many times, especially in traditional or old-school Indian societies, boys are encouraged to pursue academics and STEM fields, while girls are assigned home roles and taught this from a young age. This demotivates girls and severely restricts their aspirations.

Problem 2: Pure Theory vs. Practical intuition

Majority of science concepts are simply taught through maximum theory and minimal practical examples. Although this gets students through exams, they lose the opportunity to build intuition for visualization. Students in underprivileged communities and government schools face this even more due to lack of resources.

My Action Plan: Fulfilling The Mission
